Notre-Dame and Usher lakes are stunning to behold!

The natural beauty which surrounds us underscores how lucky we are to have this little slice of paradise and the essential need to protect and preserve it.

That is precisely the mandate of your lake association.

Please take a few moments right now to join the association as a gesture of care for the future of our lakes!
