### Annual Association Meeting - July 4th, 2026 at 4:00 PM at the Rupert Community Center

If you are able to attend, we are looking forward to seeing you on July 4th!

If you can’t attend, we're sorry you won't be able to join us. At any time if you have questions or comments for the Lake Association, please reach out. We're at [info@lacnotredame.org](mailto:info@lacnotredame.org).

Once again, this year, we are encouraging all lake users to follow the [Watercraft Guidelines](/assets/img/maps/Guidelines_Map_English.pdf) the Association’s members adopted last summer to help stop the spread of milfoil.

Please steer clear of the weeds!

See you at the lake.
