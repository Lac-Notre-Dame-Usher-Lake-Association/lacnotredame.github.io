### Watercraft Guidelines

Invasive Eurasian watermilfoil is well-established in Lac Notre-Dame. 
Today, milfoil is present throughout much of the lake because this hearty plant spreads easily and thrives in the many shallow areas of Lac Notre-Dame. A survey of residents in June 2025 found a high level of concern about milfoil’s impact on laker users and the environment. ### Resident Consultation Survey
* Printable version (PDF): [Resident Consultation Survey on Eurasian Water Milfoil - July 2025](/assets/docs/Milfoil_survey_report_E.pdf)
In July 2025, members attending the Annual meeting of the Association unanimously adopted Watercraft Guidelines to slow the spread of the weed. 
Please follow the Guidelines (stay out of the RED zones!) and ask your guests to follow them too!

* Following consultation with residents, the Lake Association has adopted Watercraft Guidelines designed to prevent the spread of milfoil on the lake.
* It’s simple: Just steer clear of the weed.
* It was not as bad in 2025, but it was still present in all of the Red Zones.
* Last summer, we knocked on every door around the lake to drop off a durable copy of the Guidelines Map which was produced to show the extent of milfoil coverage in 2024.
