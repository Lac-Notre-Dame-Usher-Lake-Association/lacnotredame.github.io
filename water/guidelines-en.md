### Watercraft Guidelines
* Milfoil is present throughout much of the lake because this hearty plant spreads easily and thrives in the many shallow areas of Lac Notre-Dame.
  
* In July 2025, members attending the Annual meeting of the Association unanimously adopted Watercraft Guidelines to slow the spread of the weed.
  
* Please follow the Guidelines (stay out of the RED zones!) and ask your guests to follow them too!
  
* A durable copy of the Guidelines map has been provided to every residence around the lake.

* Most lake users seem to be following the Guidelines!
  
* Printable version (PDF): [Watercraft Guidelines](/assets/img/maps/Guidelines_Map_English.pdf)

### Resident Consultation Survey
* A survey of residents in June 2025 found a high level of concern about milfoil’s impact on laker users and the environment.
  
* Printable version (PDF): [Resident Consultation Survey on Eurasian Water Milfoil - July 2025](/assets/docs/Milfoil_survey_report_E.pdf)
