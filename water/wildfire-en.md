### Preventing Wildfires!

* Wildfires have become a fact of life in the Gatineau. Just last year an innocent campfire came within moments of becoming a wildfire that could easily have engulfed homes and forest around Lac Notre-Dame and surrounding area.
* The fact is most wildfires are caused by human activity. You can prevent wildfires:
  * Do not light an open fire when the [Fire Danger Index](https://www.sopfeu.qc.ca/en/map/zone-gatineau/) is Very High or Extreme.
  * Sign up for a mobile ap created by the fire prevention organization SOPFEU to receive alerts about wildfires, current restrictions, and the Fire Danger Index rating for our area.
  * Ensure cigarettes or other smoking materials are dead cold before discarding them in the forest.
  * Follow [Municipality of La Pêche fire restrictions](https://www.villelapeche.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-10-Guide-feux-exterieurs-EN.png) which prohibit open fires of any kind when winds exceed 15 km/hour or when the Fire Danger Index is <r>Extreme</r>.
